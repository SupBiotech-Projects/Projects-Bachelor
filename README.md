# Bachelor Projects – Data Repository

## Overview

This repository contains data generated by Bachelor’s degree students at SupBiotech as part of their applied and supervised projects.

These projects are tutored by teachers and professors and are closely linked to ongoing research activities across multiple fields of biotechnology.

The repository provides a structured environment to store, document, and share project data in a clear and reproducible manner.

---

## Objectives of Bachelor Projects

These projects are designed to:

- Introduce students to applied biotechnology projects  
- Provide hands-on experience in experimental and data-driven approaches  
- Familiarize students with research methodologies and scientific reasoning  
- Develop skills in data organization, analysis, and interpretation  
- Encourage interaction between teaching and research activities
